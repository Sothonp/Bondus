Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Bondus helps Cambodian students prepare for the Grade 12 national exam. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect when you use Bondus, why, who else handles it, and how to ask us to delete it.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account, your study progress, and what you choose to upload.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking scripts on the site.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and everything linked to it at any time.

1. What we collect

Your accountYour name, email address, and a password (stored scrambled, so we cannot read it). Older accounts may also have a phone number. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We never see your Google password and we get no access to your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.
Your study profileThe things you tell us while setting up: your track (Science or Social Science), foreign language, grade, education level, age, target grade and university, exam year, daily study time, and subjects you want to improve.
Your progressPractice answers, diagnostic test results, points (XP), level, daily streak, your daily to-do list, the app state we save so you can continue on another device, and the date you last opened the app.
Things you sendPhotos or files you upload for an exercise, and questions you type or photos you send to the AI coach.
Technical dataYour IP address and browser details, which our servers use to keep the service secure and to limit repeated sign-in attempts. Our server logs keep this for a short time.

2. Why we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To save your progress, points and streak, and show them back to you.
  • To send you emails you need: password reset codes, and "come back and study" reminders (see section 5).
  • To answer your questions in the AI coach and to let exercise posters and admins see the work you submit.
  • To keep Bondus secure and fix problems.

3. Who else handles your data

We use these services to run Bondus. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Render: runs our server and database (in Singapore).
  • Vercel: serves the website.
  • Resend: sends our emails.
  • Google: provides "Sign in with Google".
  • AI providers: when you use the AI coach, the question you type, and any photo you send, is passed to an AI service (currently Groq and Google Gemini, with others as backups) so it can write the answer. Do not put private information in your questions or photos.

We do not sell your data and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

4. Who can see your work

Your progress is private to your account. Bondus administrators can see the exercise work you submit so they can review it. Your name and points may appear to other students if we add a leaderboard, and we will tell you before that happens.

5. Emails

We send a password reset code when you ask for one. If you have not opened the app for a few days, we may also send a friendly reminder email (at most three per absence). Every reminder has an unsubscribe link, and one click stops them.

6. Browser storage

Bondus keeps a sign-in token and your settings (such as language and dark mode) in your browser so you stay signed in. Google's sign-in button may set its own cookies. We do not use advertising cookies.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, progress, uploads and sign-in tokens. Backups made by our database provider are overwritten as they expire.

8. Your choices

  • Ask us to correct anything about you that is wrong.
  • Switch off reminder emails with the unsubscribe link in any reminder.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete your account, by emailing us (below). We will answer within 30 days.

9. Students under 18

Bondus is made for students in Grade 11 and 12 and for university students. If you are under 18, please use Bondus with the knowledge of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian can email us to ask for their child's account to be deleted.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and show a notice in the app.
